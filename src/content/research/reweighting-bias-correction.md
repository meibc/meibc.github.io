## Approach

- Apply IPF and raking to align samples with known population margins.
- Assess sensitivity to missingness and measurement error assumptions.
- Evaluate downstream impact on prevalence and intervention-effect estimates.

## Current Focus

Building a reproducible workflow for survey calibration diagnostics and reporting.
