area: "Modeling Complex Systems"
summary: This work examines maternal care delivery pathways and redesign options to improve continuity, quality, and timeliness of care.
---

## Approach

- Represent key patient flow and referral points across the service network.
- Test resource and workflow redesign scenarios under realistic constraints.
- Estimate expected effects on access, burden, and service performance.

## Current Focus

Integrating facility-level constraints and implementation feasibility into model-driven redesign recommendations.
