## Approach

- Encode causal loop structures as explicit model constraints and directional hypotheses.
- Fit dynamic relationships using available quantitative data while preserving structural priors.
- Compare unconstrained vs structure-constrained fits for interpretability and robustness.

## Current Focus

Developing a reproducible pipeline for moving from stakeholder-created system maps to parameterized models suitable for intervention scenario testing.
