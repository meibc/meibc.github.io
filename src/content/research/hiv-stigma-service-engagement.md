---
title: "HIV Stigma & Service Engagement Modeling"
area: "Quantitative Modeling of Complex Systems"
summary: This project models how social stigma, trust, and service access interact over time to shape HIV service uptake and retention.
---

## Approach

- Translate qualitative systems maps into explicit state transitions and feedback loops.
- Simulate intervention scenarios that target stigma reduction and improved service navigation.
- Compare policy options using engagement, retention, and equity-focused outcomes.

## Current Focus

Refining assumptions with stakeholder-informed pathways and preparing a calibrated implementation for scenario analysis.
