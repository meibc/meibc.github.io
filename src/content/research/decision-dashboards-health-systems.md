## Approach

- Design decision-oriented views around priority outcomes and tradeoffs.
- Expose assumptions and uncertainty in accessible, policy-relevant formats.
- Support iterative stakeholder feedback loops for usability and trust.

## Current Focus

Expanding scenario controls and simplifying uncertainty communication for non-technical audiences.
