## Approach

- Estimate comparative effectiveness under operational and budget constraints.
- Assess cost-impact tradeoffs across candidate intervention bundles.
- Produce transparent evidence summaries for funding deliberations.

## Current Focus

Improving decision rules for prioritization under uncertainty and changing implementation conditions.
