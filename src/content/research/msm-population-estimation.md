## Approach

- Combine survey, census-adjacent, and program data through model-based estimation.
- Quantify uncertainty and compare assumptions across plausible scenarios.
- Generate inputs suitable for planning models and intervention targeting.

## Current Focus

Improving transportability across geographies and stress-testing assumptions in low-data contexts.
